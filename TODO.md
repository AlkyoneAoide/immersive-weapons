## Immersive Weapons (Fabric) Weapon ideas:

### Copper Battle Axe
```
Crafting:
 - Lightning rod combined with a stick and three copper
Abilites:
 - If raining, summons lighting upon attack
 - While holding, the user is unaffected by lightning
```

### Leaching Scythe
```
Crafting:
 - Two sticks combined with a ghast tear and two nether stars
Abilities:
 - Any damage dealt you gain in health
 - If you kill a player with the Leaching Sythe you gain a heart permentently and they loose a heart permentently
```

### Soul Claymore
```
Crafting:
 - Smelt soulsand into Soul Vitric
 - Combine with a blaze rod, and two Soul Vitric
Abilities:
 - Set ablaze with blue soul flames which deal damage twice as fast as normal flames but take affect for half the time
 - Looses exponestially more durability with each strike but also becomes more powerful in terms of damage dealt
```

### Master Bee Broadsword
```
Crafting:
 - A stick combined with a bee hive and a honey block
Abilites:
 - Upon a critical hit, a angro bee is summoned on victim (7 bees max)
 - A crit also make player sticky for 3 seconds (all blocks are treated like honey blocks)
```

### Staff of the Sea
```
Crafting:
 - One conduit combined with two sticks
Abilites:
 - Holding right click will summon a storm if not present or be rid of storms if present
 - Holding left click to fire a water beam (like a guardian) cause minor fatigue (25 seconds)
```

### Mechanical crossbow
```
Crafting:
 - Crossbow combined with a dispenser, two redstone, and two sticks
Abilites:
 - A much faster less strong crossbow, the arrows do less knockback
 - Requires a redstone torch to be held in offhand
```

### Mystical bow
```
Crafting:
 - Three string combined with two sticks and an end crystal
Abilities:
 - Triggers a minor explosion on impact
```

### Grappling hook
```
Crafting:
 - A lever combined with a dispenser and a fishing rod
Abilites:
 - Its a grappling hook
 - Direct pull, 20 block reach
 - If it hits a player or entities, pulls them towards you
```
